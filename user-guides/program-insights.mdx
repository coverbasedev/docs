Program Overview is a single page for the state of your third-party risk program. You choose how to count (the level), narrow it with slicers, and open the list behind any figure.

The mistake people make most often is comparing figures taken at different levels. The **Enterprise** level counts each third party at its worst domain score across its engagements, so it reads higher than the **Third party** level for the same portfolio. Note the level when you quote a number.

## Step 1: Open the page

Open **Dashboards** in the left navigation and choose **Program Overview**. Seeing it needs permission to view dashboards.

## Step 2: Choose a level and slicers

Under **Level**, choose **Enterprise**, **Engagement** or **Third party**. The description under it says how rows are scored.

Then narrow the view with the slicers: **Tier**, **Business unit**, **Contract type**, **Control status**, **Geography** and **Risk domain**. Each one opens a searchable list. **Clear** removes every slicer at once.

The level and slicers are kept in the page's address, so copying the link shares exactly this view.

## Step 3: Read the KPIs

| KPI | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Active Engagements** | Live engagements, and how many third parties they span. |
| **High or Critical Residual** (named after your elevated levels) | Rows at your elevated residual levels, out of those scored, with the change since the start of the quarter. |
| **Reviews Past SLA** | Reviews past their SLA, out of those in flight. |
| **Issues Past Due** | Open findings past their due date, and how many block a contract. |
| **Request to Decision** | The median time from request to decision, over decisions in the last 90 days. |

Click a KPI to open the list it counts.

## Step 4: Read the heat grid

**Residual Risk · Business Unit × Domain** shows the mean residual score for each business unit and risk domain, coloured by your risk levels. Hover a cell for its mean, its risk level, how many were scored and how many sit at the elevated levels. Click a cell to open the list. **No business unit** collects records that have none.

## Step 5: Read the pipeline

**Pre-contract Pipeline · Engagements by Stage** counts requests and engagements at **Request**, **Triage**, **Inherent risk**, **Due diligence**, **Decision** and **Contract**. Click a bar to open those engagements.

## Step 6: Export and distribute

- **Export** saves the view as **Excel (.xlsx)** or **CSV**, with the level, the time it was generated, the KPIs, the heat grid and the pipeline.
- **Generate board pack** builds the board pack and downloads it when it is ready.
- The **Distribution** card lists where the program's data goes: each warehouse destination with its last sync, the board pack schedule, and scheduled dashboard emails. **Manage** opens [Data Share](/user-guides/warehouse-data-share).

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No **Program Overview** under **Dashboards** | The beta is not on for your organization, or your role cannot view dashboards. | Ask your Coverbase representative, or an admin. |
| **No residual scores yet** | No assessment has set residual risk by domain. | Complete assessments with domain residual scores. |
| **Nothing scored matches these slicers.** | The slicers exclude everything. | **Clear slicers**. |
| The opened list shows more rows than the figure | A slicer has no field on that list, so it cannot be carried over. | Expected. The page warns before you open it. |
| **Couldn't refresh this view.** | The latest figures failed to load. The page keeps the last view that loaded. | Click **Retry**. |
