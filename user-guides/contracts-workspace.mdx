The contracts module has two screens: the list page, where you see the whole portfolio at once, and the contract record, where you work one agreement. This guide covers what each part shows and what you can change.

## The contracts list page

<Frame caption="The contracts list page: key statistics, the renewal runway, and the contract table.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/contracts-portfolio.png" alt="Contracts list page with key statistics, renewal runway, and contract table" />
</Frame>

Three sections stack down the page. Read them top to bottom: the numbers, then the calendar, then the list.

### Key statistics

<Frame caption="Portfolio value and the four attention queues. Hover a card for what it counts.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/contracts-key-statistics.png" alt="Key statistics row showing portfolio value and four attention queues" />
</Frame>

Five cards sit in one row. The first is portfolio value. The other four are queues that need someone to act.

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Portfolio value** | Committed value across your contracts, in your base currency. |
| **Notice deadline in 60 days** | Contracts whose notice date falls inside the next 60 days. |
| **Clauses need a decision** | Clause review results waiting on a human verdict. |
| **Assignment clause needs re-reading** | Contracts where Radar reported a change of control at the vendor. |
| **Value disagrees with document** | Contracts whose recorded amount does not match the extracted fee. |

Hover a card to see what it counts and why it matters. The card does not expand or move, so you can hover and then click without chasing it.

Click any of the four queue cards to filter the table below to exactly those contracts. Click it again to clear the filter.

<Note>
  **Portfolio value counts one currency.** Contracts in other currencies are excluded unless your organization has pinned exchange rates. Hover the card to see which currencies were left out and how many contracts that covers.
</Note>

### Renewal runway

<Frame caption="The renewal runway. Each contract sits on its expiry date, with expired contracts in the lane above.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/contracts-renewal-runway.png" alt="Renewal runway band with contract chips placed on expiry dates" />
</Frame>

The runway is a twelve month calendar of what expires when. Each contract sits on its **expiry date**, which is the date most people recognize when they talk about a renewal.

Three lanes carry different things:

- **The band** holds contracts expiring inside the next twelve months, placed on the month they expire.
- **The row above the band** holds contracts that have already expired.
- **The line below the band** counts perpetual agreements, which have no expiry date and so cannot be placed.

Hover a chip to see the contract name, its expiry date, its notice deadline, the relationship owner if one is assigned, and where the decision stands. Click a chip to open the contract.

The dot on each chip shows how ready that renewal is:

| Dot | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Red | Review open, or nobody owns the contract |
| Amber | Owned, but no decision recorded yet |
| Green | Decision recorded |

<Tip>
  The notice deadline is the last day you can act, and it always comes before the expiry date. The runway places contracts on expiry because that is the date people search for, and carries the notice date in the hover so you still see the real deadline.
</Tip>

#### Hide or restore the runway

The runway takes vertical space. One control changes that, and it remembers your choice in this browser.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hide it">
    Hover the runway and click the **X** in its top-right corner. The whole section disappears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring it back">
    A **Show renewal runway** button appears on the header row, to the left of **Actions**. Click it and the runway returns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  These are your own view preferences, stored in your browser. They do not change anything for your colleagues, and they do not touch the contracts.
</Note>

### The contract table

Below the runway is the full list, with sorting, filtering, and pagination. Columns marked **AI** hold values Coverbase extracted from the documents rather than values someone typed.

## The contract record

<Frame caption="A contract record. Facts on the left, relationships and dates on the right.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/contracts-detail-overview.png" alt="Contract record showing details, summary, components, insights, and the right rail" />
</Frame>

Open a contract from the table, the runway, or a search result. The **Overview** tab is laid out in two columns.

The main column runs in the order you would brief someone:

1. **Details** and **Properties**, the contract's own facts
2. **Summary**, the AI read of the agreement
3. The document block: key documents, document family, contract components, and document insights
4. **Reviews**

The right rail carries context rather than content:

1. **Vendor**
2. **Lifecycle**
3. **Contract timeline**
4. **Notifications**
5. **Renewal notes**
6. **Team**

### The details card

The details card shows one row per field that has an answer. A field the contract does not carry is not drawn, so the card stays short on a thin record and grows as extraction fills it in.

Eight fields are available: description, contract value, payment terms, services, term start date, effective date, term end date, and auto renewal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a field">
    Click the **+** in the card header and pick the field. It appears as an empty row you can type into. Only fields not already shown are offered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a field from view">
    Hover the row and click the **X**. The row disappears from this contract's card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the yearly breakdown">
    Hover **Contract value**. An option to add a year-by-year breakdown appears next to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Removing a field hides it on that one contract, in your browser. It does not delete the value, does not affect other contracts, and does not change what anyone else sees. Add it back from the **+** menu at any time.
</Note>

### The documents tab

<Frame caption="The Documents tab: the document table, then key documents, the family tree, components, and insights.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/contracts-detail-documents.png" alt="Documents tab showing the document table above the document summary block" />
</Frame>

The **Documents** tab opens on the document table. Below it sits the same document block you see on the Overview tab: key documents beside the document family, then contract components, then document insights.

Both tabs render the same block, so the two views cannot disagree about the same files.
