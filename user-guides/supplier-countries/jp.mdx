A supplier whose bank account is held in Japan sees the fields below. The country is chosen on the form, and changing it changes the fields, so a supplier is never asked for a detail their country does not use.

### IBAN

Japan does not use IBANs. Accounts are identified by the fields above.

### Currency

Every account also records its currency. The form opens on JPY, the currency of Japan, and the supplier changes it for an account held in another currency.

## Tax and company registrations

| Registration | Requirement | Format | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate Number | Required | 13 characters, digits only, Japanese corporate number check digit | 13 digits. |

After a registration is saved, Coverbase checks it against Japan's National Tax Agency in the background. That check never blocks the supplier: it annotates the record once it completes, and a reviewer can retry it.

## Country rules

- Postal codes are checked against the national format. A postal code that does not match produces a warning, not a rejection.

## Where these rules come from

Oracle Fusion Cash Management 26c JP validations; NTA corporate number spec.

The rules on this page are confirmed against a primary source, so they are enforced strictly.

Configuration version `2026.10.4`. Your organization can override any of these rules for its own suppliers.
