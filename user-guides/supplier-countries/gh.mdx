A supplier whose bank account is held in Ghana sees the fields below. The country is chosen on the form, and changing it changes the fields, so a supplier is never asked for a detail their country does not use.

## Bank details

### IBAN

Ghana does not use IBANs. Accounts are identified by the fields above.

### Currency

Every account also records its currency. The form opens on GHS, the currency of Ghana, and the supplier changes it for an account held in another currency.
