A supplier whose bank account is held in Colombia sees the fields below. The country is chosen on the form, and changing it changes the fields, so a supplier is never asked for a detail their country does not use.

## Bank details

| Field | Requirement | Format | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beneficiary name | Required | up to 64 characters | The full name the account is held in, exactly as the bank has it. |

### IBAN

Colombia does not use IBANs. Accounts are identified by the fields above.

### Currency

Every account also records its currency. The form opens on COP, the currency of Colombia, and the supplier changes it for an account held in another currency.

## Additional details

Colombia collects a few fields that other countries do not. They are asked in the tax registration question, beside the number.

| Field | Requirement | Format | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nature (persona natural or jurídica) | Required | no length limit, one of: Legal entity (J), Natural person (N) |  |
| Type of entity | Required | no length limit, one of: Foreign entity, Individual, Legal |  |
| Document type | Required | no length limit, one of: 13 Cédula de ciudadanía, 31 NIT, 43 Sin identificación del exterior | The DIAN code for the document the NIT was issued against. |
| DV (verification digit) | Required | 1 character, digits only, checked against the NIT | The single digit after the hyphen in the NIT, for example, the 8 in 890903938-8. |
| Main economic activity (CIIU) | Suggested | 4 characters, digits only | The 4-digit CIIU code in box 46 of the RUT, for example, 6201. |

## Where these rules come from

Oracle Fusion Cash Management 26c CO validations; DIAN NIT checksum.

The rules on this page are confirmed against a primary source, so they are enforced strictly.

Configuration version `2026.10.3`. Your organization can override any of these rules for its own suppliers.
