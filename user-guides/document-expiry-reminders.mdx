A certificate of insurance lapses. A SOC 2 report goes stale. An ISO certificate reaches its expiry. Coverbase can email someone before each of those happens, and this guide covers the three decisions you make: **when** the email goes out, **who** receives it, and **where** you see what is coming.

## Where reminders are configured

Two places, one editor. Whichever you use, you are editing the same org-wide policy.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="From a document" icon="file">
    Open the vendor, open the document, then **Actions → Configure notifications**. The document's type is already selected.
  </Card>
  <Card title="From Documents" icon="folder-open">
    Go to **Configuration → Documents → Reminders** for every reminder your organization has, in one table.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Reminders apply to **every document of that type, across every vendor** — not to the one document you opened. Changing the certificate of insurance reminder changes it for all of them.
</Warning>

## When the reminder fires

A reminder is one or more conditions, each an offset from a date on the document:

- **30 days before** the policy expiration date
- **on** the policy expiration date
- **7 days after** it, to chase a certificate that never arrived

Which dates you can choose from depends on the document type. A certificate of insurance offers its policy expiration date, policy effective date and certificate date. A SOC report offers its publication date and audit period dates.

### Certificates covering several policies

A certificate of insurance often covers general liability, auto, umbrella and workers' compensation, each with its own expiry. Coverbase treats each coverage line as its own date, so a reminder fires ahead of each one. Two policies expiring on the same day produce one email that names both, rather than two that look like duplicates.

### Documents with no extracted date

Coverbase reads dates out of the documents it knows how to parse. For everything else — an ISO 27001 certificate, a professional licence, a tax certificate — set the date yourself: open the document, then **Actions → Set expiration date**. Once it is set, that document type can carry reminders like any other.

<Note>
  If a condition points at a date the document does not have, the editor says so. The reminder is saved, but it cannot fire until the date exists.
</Note>

## Who receives it

Each reminder has a **To** line and a **Cc** line. Add any mix of:

| Recipient | Resolves to |
| --- | --- |
| **Relationship owner** | The vendor's relationship owners, and the members of any user group set as one |
| **Risk analyst** | The vendor's risk analysts, and the members of any user group set as one |
| **Watcher** | Everyone watching the vendor |
| **Uploaded by** | Whoever uploaded that document |
| **Vendor contact** | The vendor's own contacts — an external recipient, so the picker flags it |
| A person | One named user, the same for every vendor |
| A user group | Every active member of that group |
| An email address | Anything you type, the same for every vendor |

Roles resolve per vendor when the reminder fires, so "risk analyst" means *that vendor's* risk analyst.

<Tip>
  To route certificate expiries to your risk analysts instead of the relationship owner, put **Risk analyst** on the To line and move **Relationship owner** to Cc — or take it off entirely.
</Tip>

### If nobody resolves

If no To recipient resolves for a vendor — nobody is assigned as its risk analyst, say — Coverbase sends to the Cc line instead, so the reminder still lands. If neither line resolves to anyone, no email is sent for that vendor.

## Seeing what is expiring

**Configuration → Documents → Expirations** lists every date across every document, soonest first, with:

- how long until it expires, or how long ago it passed
- which coverage line it belongs to, on a certificate covering several
- whether a reminder covers that date at all

Filter by window to answer the two questions worth asking: what has **already passed**, and what expires in the **next 30 days**.

<Note>
  A row marked **No rule** has a date nobody is being reminded about. That is usually the row to act on first.
</Note>

## When reminders go out

Once a day. A reminder set for "30 days before" goes out on the day that is exactly 30 days ahead of the date — not repeatedly through the month. Add several conditions if you want several nudges.
