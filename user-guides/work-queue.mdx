A work queue item is one task waiting on one person: a questionnaire submission to review, a risk domain review, a contract approval, an attestation, or a task a workflow raised. Most items close themselves when the record behind them is decided, so you do the work on the record and the item follows.

The mistake people make most often is looking for a request they sent on the **Items** tab. A request still waiting for the vendor's first answer has no item for you yet: the review item arrives once the vendor submits. To follow up requests that are still out, use the [Requests tab](#the-requests-tab).

## Where your work queue is

- **The top bar.** **Work queue** shows a count and opens **My Work Queue**. Select an item to open it in a panel with its title, description and instructions, and use **Back** and **Next** to move through the queue.
- **The Work Queue Items page.** **See All** at the top of **My Work Queue** opens it. Its tabs are **Items**, **Requests** (when your organization turns it on) and **Activity**, the change log across every item.
- **The Overview page.** Your items, under **Open**, **Waiting on vendor** and **Resolved**. Admins can switch between **My items** and **All items**.

## The Items tab

Pick whose items to show:

| Scope | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Mine** | Items assigned to you. |
| **My teams** | Items assigned to the people in your user groups. |
| **Everyone** | Every item in the workspace. Admins only. |

Items fall into three sections:

- **Your Turn** is work waiting on its assignee, longest waiting first.
- **Waiting** is work waiting on someone else, such as a vendor answering follow-up questions. Switch it from **All** to **Needs chasing** to see only the waits that are past their request's due date, or that have no due date and have waited more than a week.
- **Done Recently** is folded. Open it to see the latest resolved items.

Search, **Filter** and the scope apply to all three. Click a row to expand it: you see why the item is yours, its owner, its instructions, and its history. **Open item** takes you to the record. The row menu offers **Mark as resolved** (or **Approve** and **Reject** on an item that needs a decision), **Claim** and **Return to group** on a group item, **Mark as canceled**, **Reopen** on a resolved item, and **Archive**. An item tied to a record offers **Dismiss Work Queue Item** in place of **Mark as canceled**, and asks why it is no longer needed.

### What a row shows

Under its title and description, a row shows:

- The vendor, and the service the item is about beside it. A questionnaire review names the services its submission covers, and a request's item names the services of that request.
- **Your turn**, **Waiting on vendor** or **Waiting on your team**.
- The due date, as a badge.
- Who the item is assigned to. Outside **Mine**, every row names its assignee. A group item names its group, and an item you hold as someone's delegate says **On behalf of** them.

On the **Overview** page, the service sits on a line under the vendor, and on a wide screen the assignee column names the person beside their avatar.

### Due dates and reminders on a submission review

A questionnaire submission's review item takes its due date and reminders from the questionnaire template. Set them under **Actions → Configure Reviewers** on the template: **Review due** is a number of days after submission, and **Reminders** reminds the reviewer every day, every 2 days or every week. Leave them empty and the review item has no due date and sends no reminders. Reminders pause while the item waits on the vendor. See [Review due date and reminders](/user-guides/questionnaires#review-due-date-and-reminders).

### Items named after their portal

A request with several questionnaires gives each item a title that lists them. To title the items after the request instead, open the portal template under **Configuration → Portal Templates**, fill in **Display name**, and tick **Use this name in the work queue** under it. The item a colleague gets for answering the request, and the review item when the portal is reviewed as one, then carry that name, for example "Review Supplier Onboarding". It applies to requests already sent too, once their items next refresh. The option is offered on inherent risk, informational and Know-Your-Vendor templates, and needs a display name.

## Exporting the queue

Click **Export** on the **Items** tab, or on the work queue on the **Overview** page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide what goes in">
    **Apply current filters to the export** is on by default. The line under it says what that means, for example "Mine, Your turn and waiting, 2 filters". With it on, the **Items** tab exports its open items, both **Your Turn** and **Waiting**, and the **Overview** page exports the tab you are on, each with your filters and search. Turn it off to export every item in the scope, whatever its state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the format">
    **Excel** or **CSV** under **File Format**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick and order the columns">
    The list starts with **Item**, **Type**, **Status**, **Vendor**, **Service**, **Due**, **Reviewer**, **Created**, **Portal** and **Request**. Tick more (**Service Owner**, **Assessment**, **Waiting On**, **Completed**, **Link**), untick what you do not need, and move columns up or down. **Match the table** keeps only the columns the page shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the file">
    Name the file and click **Generate File**. Download it when it is ready. If you close the dialog first, a notification tells you when it is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An export holds up to 10,000 items. **Everyone** is offered only to admins, so only an admin can export the whole workspace.

## The Requests tab

The **Requests** tab lists the requests you sent or review, with their due dates, so you can follow up everything still out in one place. It lists requests, not work queue items: an inherent risk, assessment, Know-Your-Vendor or informational request appears as soon as its invitation is sent.

<Note>
  The tab is off until an admin turns it on. Open **Configuration → Portal Templates**, go to the **Request Defaults** tab, and switch on **Requests tab** under **Requests in the work queue**. It then shows for everyone in your organization.
</Note>

Pick whose requests to show:

| Scope | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Mine** | Requests you sent, and requests with a questionnaire you review. |
| **My teams** | Requests whose review is assigned to one of your user groups. |
| **Everyone** | Every request in the workspace. |

Three quick filters narrow the list, each with a count:

- **Overdue**: open requests past their due date.
- **Due this week**: open requests due in the next seven days.
- **To review**: submitted questionnaires waiting on your review.

Search and **Filter** work as on other lists. Open requests come first, soonest due first.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Service** | The services the request covers, or its name, with its reference and type under it. |
| **Vendor** | The vendor it was sent to. |
| **Sent To** | The first person it was sent to, and how many more. |
| **Sent** | When it was sent. |
| **Due** | The due date, or the day it was submitted. A request that escalated also shows **Escalated** with the date. |
| **Status** | Where the request stands. See below. |
| **Reviewer** | Who reviews it, or **Unassigned**. |

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not started** | Sent, and nothing answered yet. |
| **In progress** | At least one answer saved. |
| **In review** | Submitted and waiting on a review decision. |
| **Approved** | Approved. |
| **Changes requested** | Returned to the respondent for changes. |
| **Closed** | Closed. |

Click a row to open the request. The row menu has **Open request**, **Send reminder now**, **Change due date**, which opens the request's drawer, and **Copy portal URL**.

### Send reminder now

**Send reminder now** sends the reminder email the request's schedule would send, to the same people, straight away. The menu shows when the last one went. You can send one per request per day: after that the item is disabled with "A reminder already went out today." It works on a request that is **Not started**, **In progress** or **Changes requested**, and not on hold. The request's activity records who sent it. Sending a reminder, like changing the due date, needs permission to change requests.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What is happening |
| --- | --- |
| A request I sent is not on the **Items** tab | The review item arrives when the vendor submits. Find the request on the **Requests** tab. |
| There is no **Requests** tab | It is off for your organization. An admin turns it on under **Configuration → Portal Templates → Request Defaults**. |
| **Everyone** is missing | Only admins can see everyone's work queue items. The **Requests** tab offers **Everyone** to all. |
| **Send reminder now** is grayed out | A reminder already went out today, or the request is on hold, in review, approved or closed. |
| A review item has no due date | The questionnaire template sets no **Review due**. Set it under **Configure Reviewers**. Items opened before you set it get the due date within the hour. |
| A review item stopped reminding the reviewer | It is waiting on the vendor, for example after questions were sent back. Reminders resume when the vendor answers. |
| An item still lists each questionnaire after I ticked **Use this name in the work queue** | The template has no **Display name**, or the item has not refreshed since you saved the template. |
| My export is refused | It would hold more than 10,000 items. Narrow it with a filter or a scope. |
