A reassessment usually starts from the vendor's inherent risk as it was last time. When the relationship has changed (new data, new access, a new use), the vendor gets last year's questionnaires. The inherent risk check closes that gap: before anything goes to the vendor, the relationship owner re-answers your IRQ, prefilled with the answers you approved last cycle. The plan then picks questionnaires and control sets from the refreshed inherent risk.

The mistake people make most often is expecting the check on every assessment. It runs only on reassessments created from a plan that has it turned on: the scheduled reassessment, a Radar reassessment run, and a reassessment you start by hand with the box left checked. A reassessment means the vendor has a completed assessment, or a last assessed date brought over from another platform. A vendor's first assessment always starts right away, because there is no earlier answer to confirm.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="The reassessment is created and waits">
    The reassessment is created from its plan on schedule, from Radar, or by hand. It stays in **Created**. No vendor portal goes out and no analysis runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The relationship owner gets the IRQ">
    Coverbase emails the vendor's relationship owners a link to the IRQ, with last cycle's approved answers filled in. A reassessment for one service goes to that service's relationship owners, then the vendor's.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The owner confirms or changes each answer">
    The owner clicks **Confirm all** for answers that are still true and changes the ones that moved. They cannot submit while a carried-forward answer is unconfirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Coverbase or the assignee decides">
    If the owner kept every answer, Coverbase approves the IRQ and the assessment starts on its own. If anything changed, the assessment's assignee reviews the changes and clicks **Confirm and start assessment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The assessment starts">
    Confirming approves the IRQ onto the vendor the same way any IRQ approval does: inherent risk scores, tags and contacts update. The plan is then applied again, so its rules pick questionnaires and control sets from the new inherent risk, and document collection begins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 1: Turn on the check for a plan

Open **Configuration**, choose **Assessment Settings**, and open the plan your reassessments use. Changing a plan needs a role that can change organization settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Confirm inherent risk first">
    Under **Before a Reassessment Starts**, choose **Confirm inherent risk first**. **Start right away** keeps today's behavior.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the IRQ">
    Choose the **Inherent Risk Questionnaire** to send. Use the IRQ your intake uses, so the owner sees the same questions they answered before and their answers carry forward. If you publish a new version of it, sends use the published version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how long the owner has">
    **Due After** is the number of days before the owner's link is due. The default is 14. Portal reminders apply as they do for any IRQ.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Save plan**. Reassessments created after the save use the check. Reassessments already in progress are not changed.

<Tip>
  The IRQ goes out when the reassessment is created, which is the plan's **Document collection** offset before the reassessment date. Lengthen that offset by the owner's due window so the vendor still has the time it had before. See [Admin setup](/user-guides/admin-setup#step-7-assessment-plans-and-reassessment-cadence).
</Tip>

<Note>
  A Zero Touch plan cannot use the check, because a Zero Touch assessment contacts nobody.
</Note>

## Step 2: Follow a reassessment that is waiting

Open the assessment. The **Inherent Risk Check** card on the **Summary** tab says who the check is waiting on, when it was sent and is due, and which approved IRQ the answers came from. The status card reads **Confirm inherent risk** above the assessment's phases.

From the card you can:

- Click **Open IRQ** to read the owner's answers so far.
- Click **Send to someone else** to send the IRQ to a different person, for example when the relationship owner is out. When no relationship owner has an email address, the button reads **Send IRQ** and nothing has been sent yet.
- Click **Skip and start assessment** to start without the check. See [Skip the check](#skip-the-check).

## Step 3: Review the owner's changes

When the owner submits with changes, the card reads **Ready for your review**. It shows:

1. How many answers the owner kept and changed.
2. The inherent risk level before and after, for example **High** to **Critical**.
3. Each changed answer, with the old answer struck through above the new one.

Click **Confirm and start assessment**. The assignee also gets a review item in their work queue for the IRQ; approving it from the submission page does the same as confirming on the card.

<Warning>
  Confirming updates the vendor's inherent risk, which can change its reassessment cadence under your reassessment rules. It keeps the assessment's plan. To use a different plan for the new risk level, edit it on the assessment's **Plan** card while documents are still being collected.
</Warning>

## Skip the check

Click **Skip and start assessment**, give a reason, and confirm. The assessment starts on the vendor's current inherent risk. The IRQ stays open: if the owner answers later and you approve it, the vendor's inherent risk updates as it does for any IRQ. The card keeps who skipped and why.

## Start a reassessment by hand

Click **Start reassessment** on the vendor, or create an assessment from **Assessments**, and choose a plan that has the check. If the vendor has been assessed before, the dialog shows **Confirm inherent risk with the relationship owner first**, checked. Leave it checked for the check, or clear it to start right away.

## Work the check from MCP or the API

You can turn the check on and confirm or skip it from an MCP client, and manage plans with an API key. An MCP client reads with `query_assessments(kind="irq_review")` and `query_assessments(kind="plan_detail")`. It writes with `mutate(entity="assessment_plan", action="update")`, `mutate(entity="assessment", action="confirm_irq_review")` and `mutate(entity="assessment", action="skip_irq_review")`.

With an API key, change a plan with `PATCH /v1/assessment-plans/{assessment_plan_id}`. See [Assessment plans](/api-reference/assessment-administration#assessment-plans). The API has no route to confirm or skip the check. Confirming writes the owner's answers onto the vendor, so a person decides it.

Before an MCP client sends a write, it shows you the change and waits for you to confirm it. The [MCP tool reference](/mcp/tool-reference#assessments-evals-and-follow-ups) lists every field.

## When the check does not run

| What you see | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The card says the IRQ has not been sent | No relationship owner on the vendor (or the service) has an email address. | Add a relationship owner, or click **Send IRQ** and choose who to send it to. |
| The card says **No earlier answers to carry forward** | The vendor has no approved IRQ of this questionnaire to copy from. | Nothing to fix. The owner answers it from scratch, and the next reassessment carries their answers forward. |
| The card reads **Skipped** with a reason about engagements | The vendor's inherent risk is tracked per engagement, so a vendor-wide IRQ would not reach it. | Refresh each engagement's IRQ from its [monitoring plan](/user-guides/monitoring-plans). |
| The card reads **Skipped** saying the plan no longer asks for the check | The plan's setting was turned off after the reassessment was created. | Nothing to fix. The assessment started on current inherent risk. |
| No **Inherent Risk Check** card | The assessment was not created with the check: the plan has it off, the vendor had never been assessed, or the box was cleared when it was started by hand. | Turn the check on for the plan for future reassessments. |
| The owner cannot submit | An answer carried forward from last cycle is not confirmed yet. | The owner confirms or changes each carried-forward answer, or clicks **Confirm all**. |
| The assessment still says **Created** after confirming | The assessment is starting in the background. | Wait a moment and refresh. If your organization uses its own **Unstarted** statuses, the assessment moves to the first of them instead of starting collection. |
