  This guide is part of the [User Guides](/user-guides/overview) collection. It covers a vendor's **Internal Context** tab, the **Scoping** view on a control, and an assessment's **Scoping** tab. It sits between [creating the assessment](/user-guides/running-an-assessment#part-2-create-the-assessment) and [collecting the evidence](/user-guides/running-an-assessment#part-3-collect-the-evidence).
</Info>

The same control library often goes to very different vendors. A payments network and an outside law firm can get the same questions, but the secure development questions matter far less for the law firm. Engagement scoping lets you decide, for one assessment, which questions to emphasize, which to ask as written, which are lower priority and which are out of scope. Coverbase recommends the scope from what your team knows about the relationship, and every reason points to the row, page or intelligence section it relied on.

Your Coverbase representative turns on engagement scoping for your organization. When it is on, every new assessment is scoped first, and it starts when someone approves the scope or skips scoping.

The mistake people make most often is waiting for evidence collection on an assessment whose scope nobody has approved. The status card shows **Scoping · ready for review** until you click **Approve scope**.

## Step 1: Add internal context to the vendor

Internal context is what your own team knows about the relationship: inherent risk questionnaire (IRQ) answers, application inventory records, architecture documents, notes. The vendor never sees it, and Coverbase never uses it as evidence that the vendor has a control.

1. Open the vendor and choose **Internal Context**.
2. Click **New context**.
3. Choose how to add it:
   - **Upload files**: drop one or more PDF, Word, Excel, CSV or text files, up to 25 MB each. Before you save, each file shows what Coverbase read from it, for example **Coverbase read 19 records from 1 sheet**.
   - **From vendor documents**: pick a document already on the vendor.
   - **Write a note**: type the facts in plain text.
4. Pick what the context **Applies To**: the whole relationship or one service, and its **Source**.
5. Save.

## Step 2: Set criteria on a question (optional)

A question can carry its own rules for when it matters more or less. Criteria are kept on the control, so they apply to every assessment that asks it.

1. Open **Configuration → Controls**, open the control set, and open the control.
2. Choose the **Scoping** tab.
3. Click **Add criterion** and pick one:
   - **When conditions hold**: choose a relevance and add conditions. Choose whether **all of these hold** or **any of these holds**.
   - **When Coverbase judges a sentence true**: choose a relevance and write what must be true, for example "The vendor runs AI models that make or support decisions about our customers."
4. Click **Save criteria**.

A condition can check one of four things:

| Condition on | Holds when |
| --- | --- |
| **Context field** | A column of the workbooks uploaded as internal context matches, for example **Stores Card Numbers?** **is** **No** **in every record**. |
| **Vendor tag** | The vendor has, or does not have, the tag. |
| **Service tag** | Any service in the assessment has the tag, or none has it. |
| **Service attribute** | Any service in the assessment includes the value, or none does, for example **Data Classes** **includes** **Cardholder data**. |

Coverbase checks conditions first and then sentences, in the order listed, and the first that holds decides. A condition on a context field holds only when at least one record has that column. Questions that no criterion decides get Coverbase's own recommendation. A relevance set by an assessment plan, or picked by an analyst, still wins over a criterion.

## Step 3: Create the assessment and watch the scope run

Create the assessment as usual. Coverbase opens it on the **Scoping** tab and starts the recommendation at once. A progress bar shows how many questions are scoped. You can leave the page while it runs.

<Frame caption="A scope being recommended. The status card shows the Scoping step ahead of Collecting Documents.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/scoping-progress.png" alt="A new assessment on its Scoping tab. The status card reads Scoping 33% above Collecting Documents, Analyzing Controls and Review Results. The tab shows Recommending a scope for 15 questions with a progress bar at 5 of 15 questions scoped and four stages, two of them done." />
</Frame>

The status card shows a **Scoping** step ahead of **Collecting Documents**. While the scope waits for approval, the assessment stays at **Created** and you cannot move it to another status, except to cancel it.

Coverbase reads:

- the vendor's internal context, notes and files
- the services in the assessment and their tags
- the vendor's intelligence: security posture, financial health, compliance and legal, and radar alerts from the last year
- each question's criteria and your assessment plan's rules

The line under the summary says what was read. Click **Sources** for the list.

## Step 4: Review the recommendation

Each question shows its relevance and why. Under each reason are the sources it cites. Click a source to see the quoted words and where they sit, for example **Row 5** of a workbook, and click **Open file** to see the file.

<Frame caption="A citation opens to the quoted words and where they sit.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/scoping-citation.png" alt="A citation popover on IAM 1 for northwind-app-inventory.xlsx, Row 6, marked Internal context, never shown to the vendor, quoting Moves settled funds to merchant bank accounts each business day, with an Open file link." />
</Frame>

| Relevance | What happens in the evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| **Emphasize** | Held to a higher bar |
| **Standard** | Evaluated as written |
| **Lower priority** | A gap is recorded as an observation, or raised as an issue, as set under **Lower Priority Gaps** |
| **Out of scope** | Left out of the evaluation |

Under each question, the decision source says who decided it: Coverbase, a plan rule, the question's criteria, or an analyst. To change a question, pick another relevance and add a note. Your decision survives a **Regenerate**.

## Step 5: Add context through the chat

Use the box above the questions to tell Coverbase something about the engagement, or to ask why a question landed where it did. For example: "The wallet app never stores full card numbers. Cards are tokenized before they reach the vendor."

When the answer would change the scope, Coverbase shows a proposal: the note it would save as internal context, and each question it would move, with the reason and its sources. Nothing changes until you click **Apply**. **Dismiss** changes nothing.

The answer proposes saving that as a note and moving AI 2 from Emphasize to Standard, with its reason and two cited sources, above Dismiss and Apply buttons." />

When you apply a proposal, the note is saved on the vendor's **Internal Context** tab, marked as from the scoping chat. A question you or a workbook already decided is never moved.

## Step 6: Approve the scope

Click **Approve scope**. The dialog lists how many questions are in each relevance and what each one means for the evaluation. Click **Approve and start**. The assessment starts and Coverbase begins collecting evidence.

<Frame caption="Approving starts the assessment.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/scoping-approve-dialog.png" alt="The Approve Scope and Start the Assessment dialog: 9 Emphasize held to a higher bar, 3 Standard evaluated as written, 3 Lower priority where a gap is recorded as an observation, and 0 Out of scope left out of the evaluation, with Cancel and Approve and start." />
</Frame>

To change an approved scope, click **Reopen**. The assessment keeps running. Your changes apply from the next evaluation run once you approve the scope again.

### Skip scoping

To run an assessment without a scope, click **Skip scoping**, next to **Approve scope** or under a recommendation that is still running, and confirm. The assessment starts at once as if scoping were off: every question is asked and evaluated as written. The status card shows **Scoping skipped**, and the **Scoping** tab says who skipped it and when. To scope it after all, click **Scope this assessment**. The assessment keeps running, and an approved scope applies from the next evaluation run.

## Step 7: See what the scope changed

Once results are evaluated under the approved scope, the **Scoping** tab shows **Effect on Findings**: how many issues the results raised, how many gaps on lower priority questions were kept as observations, how many out of scope questions were left out, and how many issues the same results would have raised without the scope.

Effect on Findings reads 6 results evaluated under this scope raised 2 issues and kept 2 gaps on lower priority questions as observations. No question was left out. Without the scope, the same results would have raised 4 issues. 2 of the issues are on emphasized questions." />

Each issue, and each finding raised from one, shows **Engagement Relevance**: the question's relevance and why it matters for this vendor. A gap on a lower priority question is marked **Observation** and does not raise a finding.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The assessment stays at **Created** | The scope is not approved. | Open the **Scoping** tab and click **Approve scope**, or **Skip scoping** to run it without a scope. |
| **This file has no text Coverbase can read.** | The file is empty, or a PDF is a scan with no text layer. | Upload a text PDF or the source document. |
| A spreadsheet read fewer records than it has rows | A row with fewer than two filled cells is skipped, and so is a title row above a table. | Expected. Click the file title to see which rows were read. |
| A condition on a field never holds | No uploaded workbook for this vendor has that column. | Upload the workbook as internal context, then **Regenerate**. |
| A source shows no quote | The model's quote did not match the source word for word, so Coverbase kept the citation and dropped the quote. | Click the source to open the file at that place. |
| **Reopen the scope to apply this.** on a proposal | The scope is approved. | Click **Reopen**, then **Apply**. |
| **This scope applies on the next run** | Some results ran before the scope was approved. | Rerun the analysis. **Effect on Findings** counts only results evaluated under the scope. |
| A tag or service condition never holds | The assessment has no service with that tag or attribute, or the vendor lacks the tag. | Add the services to the assessment, or tag the vendor, then **Regenerate**. |
| A question did not move when you applied a proposal | Someone already decided that question, or its relevance changed since the proposal. | Expected. Change it by hand if you still want to. |
