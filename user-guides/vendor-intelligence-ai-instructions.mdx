AI instructions are standing guidance your organization writes once, such as "our third parties are US companies unless the intake says otherwise". Every later AI step on that section follows them for every vendor, so you stop typing the same correction on each one.

They change how Coverbase reads results for your organization. They do not change the research itself, which every organization that tracks the same company shares. The most common mistake is expecting an instruction to make the research search somewhere new: it reorders, annotates and steers the steps that run for your organization only.

## Finding it

There are two places to edit the same instructions:

- **Configuration > Vendor Intelligence > AI Instructions** lists every section, what reads its instructions, and the text your organization saved.
- On a vendor's Vendor Intelligence pages, **AI instructions** sits beside the section heading. It also appears inside **Correct with AI**, **Choose peers** and **Revise**.

The button shows a blue gear and a dot when your organization has instructions saved for that section. Each section holds up to 2,000 characters.

## What each section's instructions change

The **All vendor intelligence** instructions are read by every section below, ahead of that section's own text.

| Section | What reads the instructions | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate registrations | **Correct with AI**, which can take the country your instructions name when your note names none. The review of possible matches also reads it. | Our third parties are registered in the United States. |
| Possible registry matches | When no register record is confirmed, the possible matches are reordered and each card shows whether it fits your instructions, with a reason. The best fit is marked **Check this one first**. | Prefer the US entity over a UK company with the same name. |
| Security rating | **Revise** on the Security posture page, and the explanation on a security rating change alert. | Treat missing email authentication as the first thing to raise. |
| Security peer comparison | Which companies the vendor's security rating is compared against. Saving starts a new peer set for your organization. | Compare against US regional banks. |
| Financial health | **Revise** on the Financial health page, and the explanation on a financial health score change alert. | Call out going-concern language first. |
| Adverse media | Each finding is marked **Flagged by your instructions** or **Set aside by your instructions**, with a reason. | Litigation older than five years does not matter to us. |
| People | The same marks on the findings about the vendor's leaders and staff. | Flag any sanctions or financial crime finding about an executive. |
| Licenses | A **Your Instructions** section lists the licenses and registrations your instructions single out. | Flag any money transmitter license that is not active. |
| Dark web exposure checks | Whether a dark web post is really about you or the vendor it matched. | Posts that only explain how routing numbers work are not ours. |
| Data flow checks | Whether a vendor's answers allow training AI on your data. | Treat "may use aggregated data to improve services" as allowing training. |

## Writing instructions that work

- Name facts the item itself shows: a country, a date, a status, a category. An instruction that depends on something the record does not show leaves the item unmarked.
- Say what to prefer and what to set aside. "US companies unless the intake says otherwise" works better than "be careful with UK companies".
- Keep one rule per sentence. Long text is cut at 2,000 characters per section, and the editor stops there.

## What instructions never do

- They never change the shared research. Adverse media, people, licenses and the registry search are researched once for everyone who tracks the same company.
- They never remove, edit or rescore a finding. A finding your instructions set aside stays in the list and in every count, marked so you can see why.
- They never change another organization's pages.
- They never replace a correction made on one vendor. A note in **Correct with AI**, peers chosen with **Choose peers**, and a finding set aside by a reviewer still apply. Where a vendor's own peer guidance and your instructions disagree, the vendor's guidance wins.

## When a change takes effect

| Where | When it follows a saved change |
| --- | --- |
| Possible matches, adverse media, people, licenses | The next time the page opens. Notes appear a few seconds after the findings while the review runs. |
| Security peer comparison | A new peer selection starts the next time the page opens. |
| Revise and Correct with AI | The next time you use them. |
| Score change alerts | The next score change. Alerts already written keep their explanation. |
| Dark web and data flow checks | The next check. Exposures and flows already checked keep their result. |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| I saved instructions and nothing changed on the page | Reopen the page. If the section has no findings, or a register record is already confirmed, there is nothing for the instructions to mark. |
| A finding is unmarked even though my instruction covers it | The instruction depends on a fact the finding does not show, such as a date. Rewrite it around facts the finding lists. |
| The registry still searched the UK register | Instructions do not change the search. They rank the possible matches it returned. Use **Correct with AI** with the entity's name to search again. |
| My text was cut short | Each section holds 2,000 characters. Move shared rules to **All vendor intelligence** and keep each section's text to what is specific to it. |
| Another team member's vendor shows the marks too | Instructions are saved for your whole organization, not one user or one vendor. |
