Every vendor record starts as a name and a website. This page answers the question underneath all the others: is there a real, currently registered company behind it, and is it the one you think it is?

It runs on its own once a vendor has a website on file. You do not start it.

## Finding it

Open a vendor, go to **Vendor Intelligence**, and pick the **Registrations** tab. It is the first of the four.

## Read the verdict before the identifiers

The page leads with a match decision, because every identifier below it only means something once you know the records belong to *this* vendor.

| Verdict | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matched | A registered company was bound with high confidence | Use the identifiers. They are safe to quote in a questionnaire. |
| Needs review | Something plausible was found but not confirmed | Check the identifiers by hand before relying on them. |
| No match | Nothing credible matched | Normal for small, new, or non-corporate suppliers. Not a finding against the vendor. |

<Frame caption="An unconfirmed match. The identifiers are mostly absent and the caveats carry the page.">
  <img src="/images/user-guides/vendor-intelligence-registrations-unconfirmed.png" alt="The Registrations tab showing a plausible but unconfirmed registry match, with a caveat explaining that the identifiers should be checked by hand." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Two companies called Acme is the normal case, not the exception. A "needs review" verdict usually means exactly that, and the fix is a person spending thirty seconds on the registry link rather than a support ticket.
</Warning>

## What to do with a dissolved status

A **dissolved** status on a vendor you are actively paying is one of the highest-signal findings in the product.

It usually means one of two things. A restructure happened and nobody told you, so the entity on your contract is not the entity trading today. Or you are contracting with a company that no longer exists.

Either way the next step is the same: ask procurement which legal entity is on the current contract, and compare it to the registered name on this page.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| The page says no registration record yet | The vendor has no website on file. Add one, and validation runs on the next refresh. |
| The legal name differs from the name you use | That is expected. Trading names and registered names diverge constantly. The **also known as** list shows both. |
| Two companies are in contention | The notes section records why. Open the registry links and pick, then correct the vendor's website if the wrong one was bound. |
| A US vendor has no CIK | Only public filers have one. Its absence says nothing about the company. |
