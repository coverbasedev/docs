People intelligence screens the people who run a vendor. This page screens the company itself: what the press, the courts, regulators and sanctions lists say about it over the last five years, plus any older matter still open.

It runs on its own once a vendor has a website on file, and it runs again as part of any Zero Touch assessment that includes **Adverse media** in its scope.

## Finding it

Open a vendor, go to **Vendor Intelligence**, and pick the **Adverse Media** tab. The Vendor Intelligence overview also carries an **Adverse Media** card with the standing and a link to the full page.

## The five dimensions

Every finding is filed under one of five dimensions, and each dimension carries its own standing.

| Dimension | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Media and customer sentiment | Breach coverage, controversy, safety incidents, customer sentiment |
| Litigation exposure | Suits naming the company or a unit it owns |
| Regulatory and enforcement | Enforcement actions, regulator decisions, insolvency, debarment |
| Executive conduct and stability | A matter attaching to a named leader, or leadership churn |
| Sanctions and association | Sanctions, financial crime, bribery, corruption |

A dimension reads **Adverse findings** when it holds a material finding, **Watch** when it holds adverse findings none of which is material, **Clear** when it was searched and nothing adverse was found, and **Not screened** when no search covered it.

<Warning>
  Not screened and Clear look alike on a page and mean opposite things. A dimension whose searches did not answer is never shown as clear, and the page's standing cannot be read as a clean record while any dimension is not screened.
</Warning>

## Reading a finding

Each finding row carries the matter's severity, whether it is material, its dimension, where it got to, and whether the vendor disputes it. Open the row for the detail points the research found, the outcome in words, who the matter attaches to, and every page cited.

A finding is **material** when it is High or Medium severity and neither resolved nor dismissed. A matter the vendor won, settled, or remediated with a sign-off is shown for the record and marked **Resolved**; it does not count against the vendor.

**Disputed** means the vendor publicly contests the report. A disputed matter stays material until a court or regulator closes it.

**Favorable coverage** such as an award or a safety grade is listed in its own section. It is context for the reader and never offsets an adverse finding.

## In a Zero Touch assessment

Tick **Adverse media** on the Scope step to research it as part of the run. The report carries an **Adverse Media** subsection under Company with the standing and the way into the full page, and the Reputation roll-up reads these findings beside the people dossier and watchlist matches.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| No adverse media research yet | Use **Start research** on the page, or include Adverse media in a Zero Touch run. |
| Screening did not run | The search sources did not answer. Refresh to try again, and do not record a clean result in the meantime. |
| A finding is about another company | Open the actions menu (**...**) and pick **Correct the AI**. Choose **This is a different company** and give the right website; the page is researched again straight away. |
