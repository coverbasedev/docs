Coverbase AI can read, create and change records in almost every part of Coverbase, as you and with your permissions. Each example below is an instruction you can type, what Coverbase AI does with it, and what it stops for you to approve.

The mistake people make most often is writing an instruction that names no record. "Review the findings" leaves Coverbase AI to search. "Review Snowflake's open findings", or the same words typed on Snowflake's page, starts on the right record.

## How to read the tables

The **You approve** column uses four values. They describe a run that asks before writes, which is how a run starts unless you turn on **Don't stop to ask**.

| Value | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Nothing: read only** | The step changes nothing. |
| **Nothing: it makes the change** | A change you can undo in the product. Coverbase AI makes it without stopping, and the [Agent Ledger](/user-guides/agent-ledger) records it. An admin can set any of these to **Always asks** or **Off**. |
| **Checkpoint** | The run stops for your approval. **Approve all like this** or **Don't stop to ask** can skip it, unless an admin set it to **Always asks**. |
| **Always asks** | An archive or an action that cannot be undone. It stops for your approval under every setting. |

The details of each value are on [Controls, approvals and governance](/user-guides/coverbase-ai-agent-controls). Every example also needs the permission the matching page needs. Without it, the step is refused and the report says so.

## Playbooks

Coverbase AI picks a playbook for each instruction, or writes a custom plan when none fits. The playbooks are listed under **Playbooks** on the Coverbase AI home page, each with a ready-made instruction.

| Playbook | What it does | Where it stops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Onboard a vendor** | From a vendor name to an assessed, monitored vendor: research, intake, screening, the inherent risk questionnaire, control sets, assessment, findings and Radar | Submitting the intake, the inherent risk review, launching, a new control set, turning a detector on |
| **Inherent risk questionnaire** | Runs your inherent risk questionnaire for a vendor from research and reads the profile it produces | Submitting, the inherent risk review |
| **New assessment** | Picks control sets for a vendor, launches, waits and summarizes the results with findings | Launching, raising findings, or one batch for many vendors |
| **Zero Touch triage** | Launches Zero Touch runs for one vendor or a list and ranks what needs a full assessment | Launching, or one batch |
| **Radar monitoring** | Deploys detector templates or writes a custom detector, left off, and clears the In Alert queue | Turning a detector on, dismissing, opening a case, starting a reassessment |
| **Workflow** | Forks the workflow template that matches the instruction and sets it up, left off | Turning it on |
| **Dashboard** | Forks the dashboard template that answers the question, adds library charts, or designs custom charts | A custom dashboard or custom charts: the card lists every chart |
| **Review findings** | Reads a vendor's or an assessment's open findings and issues and says what to do | Each commitment change, anything sent to the vendor |
| **Review an assessment** | Proposes an outcome for each control that needs a person, raises findings and obligations, and works an existing assessment | Each control while you watch, one batch in the background |
| **Breach response** | Works up a filing or a Radar signal that names a vendor in a breach | Confirming the vendor, the case, the finding, the reassessment |
| **Build or edit a workflow, control set or questionnaire** | Builds a draft, shows it, applies your edits, and publishes or turns it on after you approve | Creating, turning on, publishing, every control set change, archiving |
| **Archive or turn off** | Archives a record you name or turns a workflow or detector off, and says what goes with it | Always |
| **Configuration** | Changes email and portal templates, tags, statuses, scales, reassessment rules and settings as an admin would | Every change except your own notification settings |
| **Answer a question** | Answers from the record on the page, your records and the web, with a link for every record cited | Never: it writes nothing |
| **Set up your vendor risk program** | A new organization's first setup, watched: learns how your program works, sets up controls, onboards the first vendor and launches its first assessment | Submitting, launching, a new control set |

## Questions and research

Coverbase AI answers a question without changing anything. It reads the record you are on first, then your other records, then the public web when the question reaches outside Coverbase. Each record it cites is a link, and each claim from the web names its source.

| You type | What Coverbase AI does | You approve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is still open on this assessment? (on an assessment) | Reads the assessment and answers with a link to each open item | Nothing: read only |
| Why is this finding still open? (on a finding) | Reads the finding's history and answers with a dated chain: each date, who acted and what they did, ending with what it waits on | Nothing: read only |
| What changed on Okta this quarter? | Reads the vendor's history and the records on it, and lists the changes by date | Nothing: read only |
| Has Okta reported a breach in the last 12 months? | Searches the web, counts only results about Okta, and names each source | Nothing: read only |
| What would Snowflake's inherent risk be? (no Snowflake record) | Scores your risk domains from research in shadow mode, which saves nothing, and leads with the level and the domains that drive it | Nothing: read only |
| Which of our vendors depend on AWS? | Reads your Nth parties and lists the vendors that rely on the company | Nothing: read only |
| How many High findings are overdue, by owner? | Reads the findings register with that filter and gives the counts | Nothing: read only |
| What does our Vendor risk dashboard say about Critical vendors? | Reads the dashboard and names the chart each number comes from | Nothing: read only |
| Which intake requests are waiting on review? | Lists the open intake requests | Nothing: read only |
| What has Acme uploaded through its portal? | Lists Acme's live documents with their type and upload date | Nothing: read only |
| Compare these two vendors (two vendors picked from **+**) | Builds a table of status, tier, inherent risk and open findings from the records, and names the gaps | Nothing: read only |
| List the opinion and every exception in this SOC 2 report (file attached) | Reads the report page by page and quotes each exception with its page | Nothing: read only |

## Configuration (admins)

Each change needs the permission its settings page needs. Coverbase AI reads the current value first and says the change as old against new.

| You type | What Coverbase AI does | You approve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change the subject of the vendor invitation email to "Action needed: your security review" | Rewrites the template's subject, keeping its variables | Checkpoint |
| Create a portal template for annual reviews with 14 days to respond, asking for a SOC 2 report | Creates the template, and publishes it when it is a persistent one | Checkpoint for each change |
| Add a tag group Region with the value EU | Creates the tag | Checkpoint |
| Add a custom status called Awaiting legal in the started group | Adds the status | Checkpoint |
| Set the assessment completion SLA to 30 business days | Changes the setting. An SLA that names no target is asked about with a menu of the four targets. | Checkpoint |
| Rename "vendor" to "supplier" across Coverbase | Changes the terminology setting | Checkpoint |
| Add a Very High level to our risk scale | Adds the level | Checkpoint |
| Give Priya the admin role | Refuses. Roles, permissions, members, sign-in, SSO, API keys and billing are never changed by Coverbase AI. | Not available |

## Many records at once

| You type | What Coverbase AI does | You approve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reassess every Critical vendor | Resolves the set and starts one background run per vendor, at most 25, then waits and writes one report linking each | Starting the runs is a checkpoint. Each run asks for its own checkpoints. |
| Set the Contract owner field on every vendor tagged Finance | Resolves the vendors, then proposes one change per vendor as one batch | One batch |
| Run Zero Touch on every vendor with no assessment | Resolves the vendors through the Vendors page's filters (at most 50), names them, then proposes one launch per vendor as one batch | One batch |

A set of more than 25 records for child runs, or more than 50 for a batch, is narrowed with you first.

## Getting started and standing work

| You type | What Coverbase AI does | You approve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set up my vendor risk program with me | Asks at most three questions (what you buy and the regulation you answer to, your frameworks, your first vendor), keeps the answers as memory, checks your risk domains, inherent risk questionnaire, control sets and portals, onboards the first vendor and launches its first assessment | Submitting, launching, a new control set |

To teach every later run a rule, type it to a live run starting with "always", "never" or "from now on", or add it under **Configuration > Coverbase AI Memory**. See [Memory](/user-guides/coverbase-ai-memory-and-agents#memory).

A standing agent runs a job like these on a schedule or when something happens, such as a Radar alert or an overdue finding, and a workflow step **Ask Coverbase AI to…** can hand a record to one. See [Standing agents](/user-guides/coverbase-ai-memory-and-agents#standing-agents).

## Where else you can start or answer a run

- **Slack and Microsoft Teams.** A direct message that starts with `Coverbase AI:` starts a background run as you. When a run needs you, the approval can reach you there.
- **Email.** A needs-you email has one button per option, and you can reply to it in your own words.
- **Your AI assistant.** The [MCP server](/mcp/tool-reference#coverbase-ai-and-agents) starts, answers, steers and stops runs.

## What Coverbase AI does not do yet

These modules are not available to Coverbase AI in Beta: RFPs, Inspect, risk scenarios, sending a contract for signature, and allowlisting a screening match. A run that touches one says so in its report and does the rest of the job.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The run says it cannot do a step | You lack the permission the step's page needs, or an admin set that change to **Off**. The report says which. |
| The run did a change without asking | It was a change you can undo, which runs without a checkpoint. Ask an admin to set it to **Always asks** under [What Coverbase AI May Do on Its Own](/user-guides/coverbase-ai-agent-controls#organization-policies-for-each-change). |
| The run changed the wrong record | Undo the change on the record's page, then tell the run which record you meant, or start again from the record's page. |
| The run asked about each change one by one | You were watching. In the background, a review pauses once on a batch. |
| A set was cut short | Batches hold at most 50 changes, and child runs are at most 25. The run proposes the rest after you answer. |
