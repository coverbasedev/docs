A case asks several vendors the same questions about one event. Each vendor gets an email and, if you attach a portal, a link to a short questionnaire. The portal comes from a case portal template. An organization that has never created one has nothing to attach, so set that up first.

## Before you start

You need permission to edit portal templates and to update Radar alerts. You also need a questionnaire of the kind a case portal can hold. A new organization has one named **Case inquiry**. If yours does not, create a questionnaire for cases under **Configuration → Questionnaires** before you continue.

## Step 1: Create a case portal template

1. Open **Configuration → Portal Templates** and click **New template**.
2. Choose the **Case** portal type and give the template a name, for example "Case follow-up".
3. Add a questionnaire and pick your case questionnaire. The picker lists only questionnaires a case portal can hold. An assessment questionnaire does not appear here.
4. Set **Due in (days)**. The due date on each vendor's portal counts from the day the email is sent.
5. Review the reminder schedule and save.

A portal keeps the reminder schedule it was created with. If you change the template later, portals that are already open keep the old schedule.

## Step 2: Open a case from a signal

1. Open **Radar** and select a signal that is in alert.
2. Create a case from the signal and choose the vendors it affects.

## Step 3: Send the emails

1. In the **Send Emails** step, write the subject and body.
2. Turn on the questionnaire portal link and pick your template. If you leave it off, vendors get the email with no link, even when the body mentions one.
3. Check which vendors have no contact. A vendor with no contact receives nothing. Use **Source missing contacts** on the case's vendor list to look for one.
4. Send.

You can skip sending and do it later from the case page.

## What vendors receive

Each vendor contact gets one email with your message and a link to the portal. The portal asks the questionnaire's questions and shows the due date. Replies appear on the case, one thread per vendor.

## If something looks wrong

| What you see | Likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| No template to choose in the send step | No case portal template exists yet. Do Step 1. |
| Your questionnaire is missing from the template's picker | It is not a kind a case portal can hold. |
| A vendor shows as skipped | The vendor has no contact on the case. |
| The email went out with no link | The portal link was off when you sent. |
