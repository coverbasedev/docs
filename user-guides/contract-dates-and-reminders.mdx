Every contract record carries a **Contract timeline**: the dates that matter for renewals, notice, and audits, laid out in order. Reminder emails fire off those dates. This guide explains what each date means, which ones Coverbase calculates, and — the question that brings most people here — how to stop a reminder on one contract without touching anything else.

## The timeline at a glance

Open a contract and look at the **Contract timeline** card in the sidebar. Six dates can appear:

| Date | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Effective date** | When the agreement takes legal effect. |
| **Term start** | When the current term began. |
| **Term end** | When the current term expires. |
| **Notice due to vendor** | The last day you can give notice to terminate or not renew. |
| **Internal action** | Your team's own deadline to decide, ahead of the notice date. |
| **Audit date** | When you've committed to review or audit the vendor. |

Three of the six — **Notice due to vendor**, **Internal action**, and **Audit date** — are optional. They appear when they're relevant, and you can turn them off per contract. Effective date, term start, and term end are always shown.

<Note>
  Optional doesn't mean unimportant. It means the date makes sense on some contracts and not others, so Coverbase lets you decide contract by contract.
</Note>

## Fixed dates versus calculated dates

This is the distinction behind most surprises, so it's worth getting straight.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fixed date" icon="thumbtack">
    A date you or the document states outright. It sits there until someone changes it. Renewals don't move it — a date the agreement names is a fact, and Coverbase won't rewrite a fact.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Calculated date" icon="arrows-rotate">
    A date Coverbase works out from other values. **Notice due to vendor** can be calculated as term end minus your notice period. **Internal action** is calculated as the notice date minus your lead time. Both update automatically when the contract renews.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To see which one you're on, click the date in the timeline. The editor opens on two choices: **Based on term end** (calculated) or **Fixed date**.

<Warning>
  **A calculated date can't be cleared.** If **Notice due to vendor** is set to *Based on term end*, deleting it does nothing — it's re-derived from term end minus the notice period the moment you save, and it will keep coming back for as long as those two values exist. This is working as intended, but it's the number-one source of "why won't this date go away?" To stop it appearing, hide the milestone (below) or switch it to **Fixed date** and set the date you actually want.
</Warning>

## Who gets reminded, and when

Reminders are email, and they come from two places:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Organization-wide rules">
    Set once for the whole org from the **Contracts** page. For example: "email 60, 30, and 7 days before the notice due date." Every contract inherits these unless it has its own rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contract-specific rules">
    Set on one contract from the **Notifications** card, using the gear icon. These replace the org-wide rules for that trigger on that contract — useful for a contract that needs six months of warning instead of one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Recipients are the contract's relationship owners and watchers, plus any extra addresses on the rule. The **Notifications** card shows exactly what's queued and who'll receive it.

<Tip>
  The org-wide notification settings are org-wide by design. Changing them to silence one contract silences it everywhere. Use the per-contract control below instead.
</Tip>

## Turning off a reminder for one contract

This is the per-contract off switch. It works the same whether the date is fixed or calculated, and it doesn't touch any other contract.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contract and find the timeline">
    Go to the contract record and look at the **Contract timeline** card in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the date you want to silence">
    Click **Notice due to vendor**, **Internal action**, or **Audit date**. The editor opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Hide and stop reminders">
    At the bottom of the editor, select **Hide and stop reminders**. The row disappears from the timeline and its reminder emails stop for this contract.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What that does, precisely:

- **The reminders stop for this contract only.** Your org-wide rules are untouched, and every other contract keeps reminding as before.
- **Nothing is deleted.** The date, the notice period, and the page citation from the source document all survive.
- **It's reversible.** Use **Add milestone** at the top of the timeline card to bring the date back, with its value and reminders intact.

<Note>
  Hiding **Notice due to vendor** stops both notice reminders — the notice-due reminder and the older termination-notice reminder — because they name the same deadline. You won't get one after silencing the other.

  Each milestone is a separate switch, though. **Internal action** is calculated from the notice date and keeps its own reminders, so hide it too if you want the whole deadline to go quiet.
</Note>

The **Notifications** card will tell you when this is in effect: if a date is hidden, a line at the bottom of the card names it, so a rule that looks configured but never fires is explained rather than mysterious.

### When this is the right move

The clearest case is an **estimated term end**. If the term end is your best guess rather than a date the contract states, then a notice deadline calculated from it is also a guess — and being reminded to act on a guessed deadline is noise. Hide the notice date, keep your estimated term end for planning, and add the milestone back if the term end firms up or you decide to terminate early.

Others worth knowing:

- A contract that auto-renews on terms you're happy with, where you don't intend to give notice.
- An audit date that belongs to a different team's process and shouldn't page yours.
- A contract in the middle of renegotiation, where the current dates are about to be replaced.

## Troubleshooting

| What you're seeing | What's happening |
| --- | --- |
| "I only see **Edit**, no on/off switch." | The switch is inside the editor. Click the date, then look at the bottom of the panel for **Hide and stop reminders**. |
| "It won't let me clear the date." | The date is calculated. Clearing has no meaning while term end and the notice period are populated — it re-derives on save. Hide the milestone, or switch to **Fixed date**. |
| "The date came from the uploaded document." | That doesn't lock it. Hiding the milestone overrides the extracted date, and you don't need to re-upload or edit the document. |
| "I turned it off in the org notification settings and it's still emailing." | Org-wide settings apply to every contract. To silence one contract, hide the date on that contract. |
| "I still want to see the date, just not the emails." | Today, hiding does both. If you need the date visible but silent, tell your Coverbase contact — the distinction is on our radar. |
| "I hid the notice date but I'm still getting internal action reminders." | Each milestone is its own switch. Hiding the notice date doesn't delete it, so the internal action date still calculates from it. Hide **Internal action** as well. |
