Coverbase sends two kinds of email. **Notifications** go to your own team and are driven by things happening in the platform: a finding assigned to you, an assessment ready for review, an export finishing. **Vendor emails** go outside your organization: portal invitations, reminders, follow-up questions.

Both kinds share one visual system, so a reminder to a vendor and a notification to an analyst look like they came from the same company. This guide covers what gets sent, who receives it, and the three levers you have over the appearance.

## What an email looks like

Every email is built from two pieces. The **content** is what that particular email says. The **layout** is everything around it: the background, the card, the header with your logo, the footer.

Every email that points somewhere in the platform carries a button to that exact place. A finding notification opens that finding. An assessment reminder opens that assessment. You should never have to search for the thing an email is telling you about.

## Choosing a layout

Go to **Configuration → Communications** and scroll to **Layouts**. You set two layouts independently, and the switch at the top right of that section picks which one you are editing:

- **Internal (team)** is used for notifications to your own team.
- **External (vendors)** is used for vendor-facing email.

Setting them separately is useful when you want vendor email to carry heavy branding while internal notifications stay plain, or the reverse.

Four layouts are available under **Layout style**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Branded Card">
    A bordered white card on a soft background, with your logo in the top left and the footer below the card. This is the default and the safest choice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Minimal">
    A thin accent rule across the top, then content flush against the page with no card. Lighter, closer to a plain message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Left Accent">
    A card with a solid bar of your brand color along its top edge. Use it when you want the color to carry more weight.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Plain">
    No card and no chrome, so it reads like mail from a person rather than from a system. Often the best choice for vendor email, where a heavily designed template can look like marketing and get ignored.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Alongside the layout you can set:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Use brand logo** | Puts your logo in the email header. **Update in Branding** beside it opens the organization settings where the logo is set. |
| **Use brand color** | Applies your brand color to buttons and links. Without it, buttons are near-black. |
| **Font** | Inter, Lato, Arial, Georgia, or Verdana. Inter matches the application. |
| **Footer text** | A line of your own under every email, such as a mailing address or a confidentiality note, up to 2,000 characters. |

Changes save as you make them, and the **Email Preview** on the right updates to match. **Send test to myself** delivers the preview to your own address so you can check it in a real inbox. Do this before you rely on a change.

## Applying your own branding

Branding comes from what you have already set in **Configuration → Organization → Branding**. You do not configure it a second time for email.

Two logo slots exist there, and they are used differently:

- The **Full Logo** is a wordmark, meaning a logo with your company name in it. It sits alone in the email header, since it already carries your name.
- The **Logo** is a square icon. It appears next to your organization name as a pair, the same way it does in the platform's left navigation.

On the redesigned Organization settings, both slots are on **General**: the **Wordmark** in the **Brand** section and the **Icon** in the **Identity** section.

If you have uploaded a full logo, it is used. If not, the square icon and your organization name are used together. The **Brand color** set there is what **Use brand color** applies.

The same branding applies to the header of your vendor portals and intake pages, so a vendor who receives your email and clicks through to your portal sees one consistent identity.

<Note>
  Org branding on emails and portals is released per organization. If your emails still show Coverbase branding and you would like your own applied, contact your Coverbase representative and they will enable it for you.
</Note>

This covers logo, color, and layout. To send that branded email from your own domain instead of `coverbase.ai`, see [White-labeling your vendor-facing domain](/user-guides/white-labeling).

## Who receives what

Each person controls their own notification delivery under **Account → Notifications** (choose your name in the top right, then **Account**, or **Settings** in the **Notifications** menu). Two things are separate there:

- **In-app** notifications always appear in the bell menu. They cannot be switched off, because that list is the record of what happened.
- **Email** is optional per notification type.

Some types are on by default and some are off, chosen to keep the default inbox useful rather than noisy. Anyone can change their own.

You can also choose **how** email arrives:

- **Immediate** sends each notification as it happens.
- **Digest** collects your unread notifications into one email a day, sent at the **Digest time** you pick (shown in your own time zone).

Even on digest, **Send urgent notifications immediately** lets SLA breaches, overdue reminders and direct mentions skip the digest and reach you right away.

## Vendor-facing email

These go to people outside your organization and are not controlled by anyone's personal notification settings. They use the **External** layout.

| Email | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| Portal invitation | You invite a vendor to a portal or questionnaire |
| Portal access link | A vendor requests a fresh sign-in link |
| Portal reminder | A portal is approaching or past its due date |
| Commitment reminder | A vendor has open commitments needing a response |
| Weekly vendor portal summary | A vendor has several outstanding portal requests |
| Follow-up questions | You add follow-up questions to a submission |
| Case follow-up | You reply on a case thread |
| W-9 reminder | A vendor's W-9 on file needs refreshing |
| Obligation note | You add a note to an obligation with an active portal |
| Portal passcode | A vendor asks for a one-time code to open a portal request |

The wording of portal invitations and follow-ups is yours to edit in the **Templates** list on **Configuration → Communications**, along with attachments. Sender identity is set at the top of the same page, and reminder cadence on the portal template under **Configuration → Portals**. See **Step 8** of the [Admin and setup guide](/user-guides/admin-setup).

## Contract and report email

| Email | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| Contract reminders | A contract reaches its end, renewal, audit, action, notice, or termination-notice date |

Contract reminders are configured per organization and can be turned off for a single contract without affecting the rest. ## Common questions

**An email went out looking wrong. What do I check first?**
Open **Configuration → Communications**, find **Layouts**, confirm which layout the affected side (**External (vendors)** or **Internal (team)**) is set to, and use **Send test to myself**. That delivers exactly what a recipient would get.

**Someone says they are not getting emails.**
Check their **Account → Notifications** page first, since the setting is per person. If they are on digest, the email is batched rather than missing. Also check whether the address has bounced: a hard bounce suppresses future sends, and whoever triggered the original email receives an "Email failed to deliver" notification.

**Can I turn off email for my whole organization?**
Not in one switch. Email is a per-person setting, so one person's preferences never change what a colleague receives.

**Our logo looks stretched or cropped.**
Check which slot it is in. The **Logo** slot (**Icon** on the redesigned settings) expects a square image and the **Full Logo** slot (**Wordmark**) expects a wide logotype. A wide logo in the square slot is the usual cause.
