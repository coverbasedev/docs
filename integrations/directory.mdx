Coverbase connects with the tools your security, risk, and procurement teams already run. This directory lists the external systems available on the **Configuration → External Integrations** page in the dashboard, grouped by category. Some integrations are self-serve (you configure them yourself with an API key or credentials), while others are enabled by the Coverbase team during onboarding.

<Info>
  This directory reflects the integration catalog in the dashboard. For how integrations behave end to end (inbound API, workflow engine, and outbound webhooks), see [Integration Workflows](/integrations/overview). Platforms with a deep-dive guide (architecture diagrams, data mapping, trigger model) are linked below; see the [platform-specific guides](/integrations/guides/processunity). To connect a system not listed here, [talk to your Coverbase team](https://www.coverbase.com/contact).
</Info>

## How to read this directory

- **A linked name** has a built-in connector with its own guide, or is read by a documented Coverbase feature (the link says which).
- **A name without a link** is listed in the dashboard catalog, but Coverbase has no built-in connector for it today. Its card on **Configuration → External Integrations** explains that Coverbase can build the integration and how to reach your Coverbase representative; it does not connect anything. [Talk to your Coverbase team](https://www.coverbase.com/contact), or connect it yourself through the [public API and webhooks](/integrations/guides/integration-platforms).
- **Beta** connectors (Coupa, SAP Ariba, Ironclad, Icertis, Microsoft Teams, Bitsight, SecurityScorecard, Rapid Ratings, Moody's Analytics, ISNetworld, Avetta, EcoVadis, and the Snowflake, Amazon S3 and BigQuery destinations) are part of the third-party lifecycle beta and have not yet been validated against a live customer tenant. Each guide says so.


## Cybersecurity & threat intelligence

Outside-in security ratings, attack-surface, and threat-intelligence sources that enrich Vendor Intelligence.

[Shodan](/user-guides/security-intelligence), [Censys](/user-guides/security-intelligence), [Have I Been Pwned](/user-guides/dark-web-monitoring), Axio, [Bitsight](/integrations/guides/bitsight), [Black Kite](/user-guides/black-kite-monitoring), CyberGRX, Panorays, Recorded Future, RiskRecon, [SecurityScorecard](/integrations/guides/securityscorecard), UpGuard, Mandiant, IONIX, Cyberhaven.

## AI Governance & Safety

AI governance, AI security, and model-risk platforms for evaluating vendors that build or depend on AI systems, a natural companion to the [AIUC-1, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act, OWASP LLM, and MITRE ATLAS control sets](/control-library#ai-governance).

Credo AI, Holistic AI, FairNow, Lakera, Robust Intelligence, HiddenLayer, Protect AI, Cranium, Lasso Security, WitnessAI.

## SaaS Security Posture Management (SSPM)

Identity, configuration, and posture signals from your SaaS estate.

[Okta](/user-guides/internal-controls-monitoring), FalconShield (Adaptive Shield), AppOmni, Grip Security, Obsidian Security, Valence Security (configured on its card), Nudge Security.

## Identity & single sign-on (SSO)

Identity providers Coverbase reads users, application assignments, and access data from.

[Microsoft Entra ID](/user-guides/internal-controls-monitoring), Google Workspace, AWS IAM Identity Center, OneLogin, Ping Identity, JumpCloud, Cisco Duo, Auth0, CyberArk, SailPoint, Saviynt. Okta is listed under SaaS Security Posture Management above.

<Note>
  These are the identity systems Coverbase **reads from**, for access reviews and application inspection. Signing **in to** Coverbase is a separate capability: SAML 2.0 enterprise SSO, which inherits your identity provider's MFA and conditional-access policy, with just-in-time provisioning and an API for IGA-driven joiner/mover/leaver lifecycle. See [Identity and access management](/security/identity-and-access).
</Note>

## HR & workforce management (HRIS)

HR and payroll systems that supply employee, contractor, and org data for ownership and access reviews.

Workday, BambooHR, ADP, UKG, Rippling, Gusto, Paychex, Paycom, Namely, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM Cloud, Dayforce, HiBob, Deel.

## GRC & risk management platforms

Governance, risk, compliance, and third-party risk management systems Coverbase exchanges data with. Coverbase also runs the internal side of enterprise risk natively: see [Internal control monitoring](/products/internal-controls-monitoring) for evaluating the controls inside your own applications on the same control model, risk domains, scales, findings and dashboards as your third-party program.

Archer, AuditBoard, Diligent, Fusion Risk Management, LogicGate, LogicManager, MetricStream, NAVEX Global, [OneTrust](/integrations/guides/onetrust), Prevalent, [ProcessUnity](/integrations/guides/processunity), Riskonnect, Resolver, SAI360, [ServiceNow GRC](/integrations/guides/servicenow), Vendor Insight, Vendor Risk, Venminder, Workiva, [Aravo](/integrations/guides/aravo), Whistic, VISO TRUST, Certa, Graphite Connect, Supply Wisdom, Ncontracts.

<Note>
  Archer and ServiceNow IRM risk and issue sync, and a push into a general enterprise risk register, are not built in. The [ServiceNow integration](/integrations/guides/servicenow) covers ServiceNow VRM and ITSM records. For other registers, see [pushing to an enterprise risk register](/integrations/guides/integration-platforms#pushing-to-an-enterprise-risk-register).
</Note>

## Compliance Automation & Trust Management

Continuous-compliance and trust-management platforms that automate control monitoring and evidence collection.

Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, Thoropass, Hyperproof, Sprinto, Conveyor, Vendict. [SafeBase](/integrations/guides/safebase) trust centers are read automatically during vendor enrichment, so SafeBase has no card on the page.

## Financial crime & AML

Anti-money laundering, fraud, and financial-crime platforms that risk teams at banks, credit unions, and fintechs run alongside Coverbase.

NICE Actimize, Nasdaq Verafin, Oracle Financial Crime and Compliance Management, SAS Anti-Money Laundering, Quantexa, Feedzai, Featurespace, Fenergo, Unit21, Hummingbird, Sardine, Alloy, Napier AI, Silent Eight, Chainalysis.

## Privacy & data governance

Data discovery, consent, and data-subject-request platforms that govern the personal data your vendors process.

Securiti, BigID, Transcend.

## Document & contract lifecycle management

Contract and document systems Coverbase reads obligations from and writes back to.

Adobe Acrobat Sign, Agiloft, CobbleStone, Concord, Conga, ContractPodAi, [DocuSign](/integrations/guides/docusign), Dropbox Sign, Evisort, Gatekeeper, Harvey, [Icertis](/integrations/guides/icertis), [Ironclad](/integrations/guides/ironclad), Juro, LinkSquares, Luminance, Malbek, Onit, SirionLabs, SpotDraft.

<Note>
  The [DocuSign integration](/integrations/guides/docusign) initiates execution from a Coverbase document, routes countersignature through ordered signer slots, tracks envelope status on the record, and keeps the executed agreement attached to the third party it belongs to.
</Note>

## Procurement & supplier management

Procurement, sourcing, and supplier-management platforms that drive vendor onboarding and spend.

[SAP Ariba](/integrations/guides/sap-ariba), [Coupa](/integrations/guides/coupa), [Workday Strategic Sourcing](/integrations/guides/workday-strategic-sourcing), GEP SMART, Ivalua, Jaggaer, Levelpath, Lio, NetSuite, Omnea, ORO Labs, Zip, Tropic, Vendr, Sastrify.

## Supply chain & nth-party risk

Multi-tier supplier mapping, disruption sensing, and ownership intelligence for risk that sits beyond your direct vendors.

Interos, Exiger, Resilinc, Everstream Analytics, Sayari.

## IT service management

Ticketing and service-management systems for routing findings and tasks.

Freshservice, [Jira](/integrations/guides/jira), [ServiceNow ITSM](/integrations/guides/servicenow).

## Communication & collaboration

Messaging workspaces Coverbase connects to.

[Slack](/integrations/guides/slack), [Microsoft Teams](/integrations/guides/microsoft-teams) (beta).

## Sustainability & ESG

ESG ratings and sustainability-risk sources.

CyberVadis, [EcoVadis](/integrations/guides/ecovadis), ISS ESG, Sustainalytics.

## Financial & business data

Company, credit, and ownership data that enriches vendor records and Vendor Intelligence.

Bureau van Dijk (Orbis), [Crunchbase](/products/financial-health-score), [Dun & Bradstreet](/products/vendor-intelligence), [Moody's Analytics](/integrations/guides/moodys), [PitchBook](/products/financial-health-score), [Rapid Ratings](/integrations/guides/rapidratings), S&P Global.

## Regulatory & compliance data

Sanctions, enforcement, and public-registry sources used for screening and due diligence.

[CFPB enforcement actions](/products/source-library), ComplyAdvantage, Dow Jones Risk & Compliance, [FDIC](/products/source-library), [FinCEN enforcement actions](/products/source-library), GBG, [OFAC](/user-guides/sanctions-screening), [OpenCorporates](/products/corporate-registrations), [SEC EDGAR](/user-guides/breach-notification-monitoring).

## Risk intelligence & analytics

Screening, adverse-media, and identity-verification data for third-party due diligence.

Bitsight Security Scorecard, Kroll, [LexisNexis](/products/source-library), [LSEG (Refinitiv World-Check)](/products/source-library), [Refinitiv World-Check](/products/source-library), Trulioo, UpGuard, RiskRecon by Mastercard.

## Tax & VAT validation

Registries Coverbase validates vendor tax and VAT identifiers against.

SAP, Sovos (Convey), [VIES](/user-guides/supplier-information).

## Contractor prequalification

Contractor safety, insurance and compliance networks that feed the [Certificate Vault](/products/certificate-vault). These appear on the page for organizations in the third-party lifecycle beta.

[ISNetworld](/integrations/guides/isnetworld), [Avetta](/integrations/guides/avetta).

## Data warehouses

Destinations for [warehouse data share](/products/warehouse-data-share), configured on **Configuration → Data Share** rather than on the External Integrations page.

[Snowflake](/integrations/guides/snowflake), [Amazon S3](/integrations/guides/amazon-s3), [BigQuery](/integrations/guides/bigquery).

## Integration platforms and API access

Workato, MuleSoft and Boomi connect through Coverbase's starting points (a Workato custom connector, a Mule 4 template and a Boomi recipe) built on [OAuth 2.0 client credentials](/integrations/guides/oauth-client-credentials), the REST API and signed webhooks. They are templates rather than certified marketplace connectors: see [Integration platforms](/integrations/guides/integration-platforms).
