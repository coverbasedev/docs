Coverbase connects with the tools your security, risk, and procurement teams already run. This directory lists the external systems available on the **Configuration → External integrations** page in the dashboard, grouped by category. Some integrations are self-serve (you configure them yourself with an API key or credentials), while others are enabled by the Coverbase team during onboarding.

<Info>
  This directory reflects the integration catalog in the dashboard. For how integrations behave end to end (inbound API, workflow engine, and outbound webhooks), see [Integration Workflows](/integrations/overview). To connect a system not listed here, [talk to your Coverbase team](https://www.coverbase.com/contact).
</Info>

## Cybersecurity & threat intelligence

Outside-in security ratings, attack-surface, and threat-intelligence sources that enrich Vendor Intelligence.

Shodan, Censys, Have I Been Pwned, Axio, Bitsight, Black Kite, CyberGRX, Panorays, Recorded Future, RiskRecon (Mastercard), SecurityScorecard, UpGuard, Mandiant, IONIX, Cyberhaven.

## AI Governance & Safety

AI governance, AI security, and model-risk platforms for evaluating vendors that build or depend on AI systems, a natural companion to the [AIUC-1, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act, OWASP LLM, and MITRE ATLAS control sets](/control-library#ai-governance).

Credo AI, Holistic AI, FairNow, Lakera, Robust Intelligence, HiddenLayer, Protect AI, Cranium, Lasso Security, WitnessAI.

## SaaS Security Posture Management (SSPM)

Identity, configuration, and posture signals from your SaaS estate.

Okta, FalconShield (Adaptive Shield), AppOmni, Grip Security, Obsidian Security, Valence Security, Nudge Security.

## Identity & single sign-on (SSO)

Identity providers Coverbase reads users, application assignments, and access data from.

Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, AWS IAM Identity Center, OneLogin, Ping Identity, JumpCloud, Cisco Duo, Auth0, CyberArk, SailPoint, Saviynt.

## HR & workforce management (HRIS)

HR and payroll systems that supply employee, contractor, and org data for ownership and access reviews.

Workday, BambooHR, ADP, UKG, Rippling, Gusto, Paychex, Paycom, Namely, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM Cloud, Dayforce, HiBob, Deel.

## GRC & risk management platforms

Governance, risk, compliance, and third-party risk management systems Coverbase exchanges data with.

Archer, AuditBoard, Diligent, Fusion Risk Management, LogicGate, LogicManager, MetricStream, NAVEX Global, OneTrust, Prevalent, ProcessUnity, Riskonnect, Resolver, SAI360, ServiceNow GRC, Vendor Insight, Vendor Risk, Venminder, Workiva, Aravo, Whistic, VISO TRUST, Certa, Graphite Connect, Supply Wisdom, Ncontracts.

## Compliance Automation & Trust Management

Continuous-compliance and trust-management platforms that automate control monitoring and evidence collection.

Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, Thoropass, Hyperproof, Sprinto, Conveyor, Vendict.

## Financial crime & AML

Anti-money laundering, fraud, and financial-crime platforms that risk teams at banks, credit unions, and fintechs run alongside Coverbase.

NICE Actimize, Nasdaq Verafin, Oracle Financial Crime and Compliance Management, SAS Anti-Money Laundering, Quantexa, Feedzai, Featurespace, Fenergo, Unit21, Hummingbird, Sardine, Alloy, Napier AI, Silent Eight, Chainalysis.

## Privacy & data governance

Data discovery, consent, and data-subject-request platforms that govern the personal data your vendors process.

Securiti, BigID, Transcend.

## Document & contract lifecycle management

Contract and document systems Coverbase reads obligations from and writes back to.

Adobe Acrobat Sign, Agiloft, CobbleStone, Concord, Conga, ContractPodAi, DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, Evisort, Gatekeeper, Harvey, Icertis, Ironclad, Juro, LinkSquares, Luminance, Malbek, Onit, SirionLabs, SpotDraft.

## Procurement & supplier management

Procurement, sourcing, and supplier-management platforms that drive vendor onboarding and spend.

SAP Ariba, Coupa, GEP SMART, Ivalua, Jaggaer, Levelpath, Lio, NetSuite, Omnea, ORO Labs, Zip, Tropic, Vendr, Sastrify.

## Supply chain & nth-party risk

Multi-tier supplier mapping, disruption sensing, and ownership intelligence for risk that sits beyond your direct vendors.

Interos, Exiger, Resilinc, Everstream Analytics, Sayari.

## IT service management

Ticketing and service-management systems for routing findings and tasks.

Freshservice, Jira, ServiceNow ITSM.

## Communication & collaboration

Channels Coverbase sends notifications and approvals to.

Slack.

## Sustainability & ESG

ESG ratings and sustainability-risk sources.

CyberVadis, EcoVadis, ISS ESG, Sustainalytics.

## Financial & business data

Company, credit, and ownership data that enriches vendor records and Vendor Intelligence.

Bureau van Dijk (Orbis), Crunchbase, Dun & Bradstreet, Moody's Analytics, PitchBook, Rapid Ratings, S&P Global.

## Regulatory & compliance data

Sanctions, enforcement, and public-registry sources used for screening and due diligence.

CFPB enforcement actions, ComplyAdvantage, Dow Jones Risk & Compliance, FDIC, FinCEN enforcement actions, GBG, OFAC, OpenCorporates, SEC EDGAR.

## Risk intelligence & analytics

Screening, adverse-media, and identity-verification data for third-party due diligence.

Bitsight Security Scorecard, Kroll, LexisNexis, LSEG (Refinitiv World-Check), Refinitiv World-Check, Trulioo, UpGuard, RiskRecon by Mastercard.

## Tax & VAT validation

Registries Coverbase validates vendor tax and VAT identifiers against.

SAP, Sovos (Convey), VIES.
