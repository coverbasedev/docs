Workflows in Coverbase are built in a no-code designer in the **Workflows** section of the dashboard, by your admins, at any time. Onboarding is a head start rather than a dependency: Coverbase sits with you and builds the first workflow set from your existing process documentation and integration targets, and hands it over as ordinary editable definitions.

<Info>
  There is no read-only stage and no configuration-only tier. Every trigger, condition, action and webhook Coverbase builds with you is one your admins can edit, clone, disable or replace, from the dashboard or the API, without involving Coverbase support. The same is true of control sets, questionnaires, statuses, approval options, risk domains, scales, custom fields and terminology. See [Configuring your data model](/user-guides/data-model-configuration).
</Info>

## What you configure, and what we build with you

| You, self-serve | Coverbase, with you |
| --- | --- |
| Workflows: triggers, conditions, actions, review gates and approval routing | The first workflow set, built from your process documentation during onboarding |
| Control sets, questionnaires, clause sets, report templates | Advice on which library frameworks to fork for your regulatory profile |
| Custom fields, statuses, approval options, scales, risk domains, tags, terminology | - |
| Users, roles, user groups, out-of-office routing, SSO | - |
| API keys, webhooks, and API-key integrations such as DocuSign, Slack and Jira | - |
| - | Deep bidirectional connectors to a GRC, ERP or CLM system of record, where field mapping to your instance is genuine integration work |

## What an integration delivery looks like

Platform integrations are stood up **sandbox-first**: the connector is configured and verified end to end against your platform's sandbox or sub-production tenant - including live read-back of what actually persisted - before production credentials are exchanged. A typical delivery runs:

1. **Credentials and scoping** - you provision a scoped integration identity (OAuth client, integration user, or API token) for the sandbox tenant; secrets are held in Coverbase's secrets manager, never in configuration.
2. **Mapping workshop** - your field vocabulary (assessment types, ratings, statuses, custom attributes) is mapped once onto Coverbase's model, with tenant-specific identifiers recorded in configuration.
3. **Sandbox verification** - full lifecycle runs against the sandbox, verified by reading the results back from your platform.
4. **Production cutover** - production credentials swap in; the mapping carries over unchanged.
