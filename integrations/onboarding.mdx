## What an integration delivery looks like

Platform integrations are stood up **sandbox-first**: the connector is configured and verified end to end against your platform's sandbox or sub-production tenant — including live read-back of what actually persisted — before production credentials are exchanged. A typical delivery runs:

1. **Credentials and scoping** — you provision a scoped integration identity (OAuth client, integration user, or API token) for the sandbox tenant; secrets are held in Coverbase's secrets manager, never in configuration.
2. **Mapping workshop** — your field vocabulary (assessment types, ratings, statuses, custom attributes) is mapped once onto Coverbase's model, with tenant-specific identifiers recorded in configuration.
3. **Sandbox verification** — full lifecycle runs against the sandbox, verified by reading the results back from your platform.
4. **Production cutover** — production credentials swap in; the mapping carries over unchanged.
