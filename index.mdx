## Building on Coverbase

Three integration surfaces. Each works on its own, and you can combine them.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Export API" icon="arrow-up-from-bracket" href="/export-api-concepts">
    Pull vendor, assessment and control data out of Coverbase into your dashboards, BI tools, and remediation workflows.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Import API" icon="arrow-down-to-bracket" href="/import-api">
    Push vendor, assessment and service data in from your ERP, CMDB, legacy GRC platform, or service catalog.
  </Card>

  <Card title="MCP server" icon="robot" href="/mcp/overview">
    Connect Claude, Cursor, or any MCP client and work with your TPRM program through conversation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an API key">
    Ask a Coverbase admin in your organization to provision one. See [API keys](/api-reference/api-keys).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store it properly">
    Keep it in a secrets manager. Never commit a key to source control or embed it in client-side code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Call `/v1/utils/authtest` with your bearer token to check connectivity, key validity, and firewall rules before wiring anything up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the concepts page for your surface">
    Then jump to the reference for the endpoints you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>
