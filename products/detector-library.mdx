Supplier Radar ships with a library of ready-to-use detector templates. Each template becomes a live detector on your organization when you enable it from the dashboard (**New detector → From detector library** on the Radar page, or **Add detector** under **Configuration → Radar → Detectors**). It watches your sources for events that match its focus, enriches matches with vendor context, and routes them into triage. This page documents each built-in template so you can decide what to turn on.

The library currently includes **94 detector templates**. The dialog filters them by **Recommended** and by segment: **Cybersecurity**, **Financial**, **Operational**, **Legal**, **Compliance**, **Reputational**, **Contractual**, **ESG**, **Corporate**, **Geopolitical & External** and **AI Risk**. The **Corporate** segment lists no templates today. Most templates are *standard* detectors that read a natural-language goal and severity guidance. One is a *watchlist* detector that screens vendors against connected sanctions lists, and five are *Black Kite* detectors that read your Black Kite portfolio directly, with no AI step.

<Note>
  Detector templates are a starting point. Enabling a standard or watchlist template also adds the library sources it names to your organization and scopes the new detector to them, so it works without further setup; the preview in the dialog lists those sources. Once applied, every detector's instructions, severity threshold, category filters, sources, and reviewers are fully editable in the dashboard, and detectors learn from dismissed alerts. See [Supplier Radar](/products/supplier-radar) for the product overview and [Radar API](/api-reference/radar) for programmatic access.
</Note>

## Severity levels

Each template ships with a default severity threshold. Detectors surface matches at or above their threshold; you can raise or lower it per detector.

| Severity | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| High | Confirmed, material impact: active exploitation, confirmed breach, insolvency, sanctions |
| Medium | Credible, significant events warranting review |
| Low | Early or lower-impact signals worth monitoring |
| Informational | Contextual updates (e.g. ownership changes) tracked without urgency |

### Black Kite templates

These five read your Black Kite portfolio against fixed thresholds and carry a **Black Kite** badge in the library. They can be enabled only once a Black Kite portfolio source is connected; see the [Black Kite monitoring guide](/user-guides/black-kite-monitoring).

| Detector | Default severity | What it flags |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exploited Vulnerabilities | High | A CISA KEV-listed vulnerability Black Kite found on a vendor's internet-facing infrastructure, with no susceptibility threshold |
| Critical Exploitable CVEs | High | Exploited vulnerabilities scoring CVSS 9.0 or higher with at least a 50% predicted chance of exploitation |
| Vulnerability Campaign Exposure | High | Black Kite focus tags naming a vendor as exposed to a campaign, such as a mass-exploited flaw or a ransomware wave |
| Ransomware-Susceptible Vendor Exposure | High | Exploited vulnerabilities and focus tags, only for vendors scoring above 0.5 on ransomware susceptibility |
| Highest Susceptibility Watch | High | The same signals, only for vendors scoring above 0.8 on ransomware susceptibility |
