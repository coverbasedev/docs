Coverbase assembles a vendor's risk picture from three layers of intelligence: **point-in-time due-diligence blocks** queried during onboarding and periodic review, **continuous-monitoring feeds** (Supplier Radar) that surface emerging signals in real time, and **partner integrations** you can connect to deepen coverage. This page catalogs every source across all three layers.

The library spans **90+ sources**, grouped into three tiers by how you get access. Use the tabs below to filter by tier, or read the tier definitions first.

## What the tiers mean

| Tier | What it means | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core** | Live and included in the platform today, at no additional cost or setup. | Coverbase operates the connection for you: public and free-tier feeds, or Coverbase's own keys. Nothing to configure. |
| **Optional** | Coverbase has a built API connection, but the feed runs on **your** subscription. | You contract with the provider directly and supply the API keys; Coverbase plugs them in. |
| **Premium** | Available through a Coverbase paid add-on or **reseller / partner** tier. | Access is arranged through Coverbase's commercial relationship with the provider; no self-serve key required from you. |

<Note>
  Sources are a starting point. You can add your own feeds, pages, or APIs alongside the built-in ones, and every source's scope (and the [detectors](/products/detector-library) that read it) is configurable in the dashboard. See [Supplier Radar](/products/supplier-radar) for the continuous-monitoring product and [Radar API](/api-reference/radar) for programmatic access.
</Note>
