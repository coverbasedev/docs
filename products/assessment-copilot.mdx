Assessment Copilot replaces manual, spreadsheet-driven workflows with a programmatic pipeline. It generates custom questionnaires, extracts controls from uploaded evidence, evaluates vendor responses, and drafts follow-ups for the gaps, routing the cases the evidence cannot settle to human reviewers.

## What it does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Custom questionnaire generation" icon="list-check">
    Generate vendor questionnaires scoped to the control sets you care about, the vendor's risk tier, and the services in scope.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Control extraction from evidence" icon="file-magnifying-glass">
    Parse SOC 2 reports, ISO certifications, pen test summaries, and policy documents to extract control evidence automatically.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Response analysis" icon="bolt">
    Evaluate every control against the vendor's answers and documents, flagging weaknesses as issues and marking what the evidence cannot settle for review rather than guessing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Follow-ups and reassessments" icon="code-compare">
    Draft targeted follow-up questionnaires from the issues an assessment raises, flag residual risk for reviewer attention, and schedule the next reassessment from the vendor's risk level.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Evidence quality controls

Copilot researches the public web when a control can't be answered from the documents on file. That evidence is not taken on trust: every candidate page is graded for **publisher accountability** (Unreliable, Reputable, or Authoritative), and anything below the minimum you configured is discarded before the evaluating model sees it. Accepted citations carry their grade, the reason for it, and the page's age at the time it was cited, both in the UI and in the Excel export.

## Common workflows

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Onboarding pipeline integration" defaultOpen icon="inbox-in">
    Wire Copilot into your vendor onboarding flow. When a new vendor enters intake, trigger a Copilot assessment scoped to the appropriate control set and tier. Pull results via Export API and surface them in your procurement system before contract execution.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Continuous compliance" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Schedule reassessments on a recurring cadence for each risk level (annual, semi-annual, or any interval), or trigger one from an event such as a Radar alert or a jump in the vendor's risk score. Each reassessment evaluates the controls again against the latest evidence.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Human-in-the-loop review" icon="user-check">
    Set each control set's strictness to decide how much the evaluation may infer on the vendor's behalf, and route assessments to named reviewers for approval. An evaluation the evidence cannot settle is marked for review rather than guessed, and issues surface for a person to accept, dismiss or follow up.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
