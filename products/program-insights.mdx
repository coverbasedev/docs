**Program Overview** answers the questions a TPRM Office is asked every month: how many engagements are live, how much residual risk sits at the top of the scale and where, what is stuck in the pipeline before contract, and how long a request takes to reach a decision. Every figure opens the list behind it.

## What it shows

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Five KPIs" icon="gauge">
    **Active Engagements** across how many third parties; third parties or engagements at your elevated residual risk levels (such as High or Critical), with the change this quarter; **Reviews Past SLA** out of those in flight; **Issues Past Due**, with how many block a contract; and the median **Request to Decision** time over the last 90 days.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Residual risk by business unit and domain" icon="table-cells">
    A heat grid of the mean residual score for each business unit and risk domain, coloured by your risk levels, with how many were scored and how many sit at the elevated levels.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pre-contract pipeline" icon="filter">
    Requests and engagements by stage, from **Request** through **Triage**, **Inherent risk**, **Due diligence** and **Decision** to **Contract**, until they reach a signed contract.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Levels and slicers" icon="sliders">
    View the program at the **Enterprise**, **Engagement** or **Third party** level, and slice it by tier, business unit, contract type, control status, geography and risk domain. The view is held in the link, so sharing the link shares the view.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Drill-downs" icon="arrow-pointer">
    Each KPI, heat grid cell and pipeline stage opens the matching vendor, engagement or finding list, filtered to what it counts.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Export and distribution" icon="file-export">
    Export the view to Excel or CSV, generate the board pack, and see on the **Distribution** card where your program's data goes: warehouse destinations, the board pack schedule and scheduled dashboard emails.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The three levels

| Level | Each row is |
| --- | --- |
| **Enterprise** | A third party at its worst: per domain, the higher of its own score and any of its engagements' scores. |
| **Engagement** | A live engagement, scored on its own. |
| **Third party** | A third party, scored by its rolled-up risk profile. |

## When a list shows more than the figure

When every slicer has a field on the list a figure opens, the list is filtered the same way. Otherwise the drill names the rows by ID, up to 100; past that, the list shows a superset of what the figure counted, and the page says so before you open it.

## Drill into a business unit or an engagement

From the whole program you can drill into one business unit, which narrows every figure to it and lists its third parties or engagements as the grid's rows, and then into one engagement. The drill is held in the link with the level and slicers.
