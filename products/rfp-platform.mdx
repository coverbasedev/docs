RFP Platform brings vendor selection into the same risk-aware program as the rest of Coverbase. Built for regulated industries, it embeds risk, compliance, security, and legal evaluation directly into the request-for-proposal process, so the diligence that normally happens *after* a vendor is chosen instead shapes the decision while it's still being made.

## What it does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI-drafted RFPs" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Describe what you need in a sentence. The wizard asks the clarifying questions, drafts the RFP, proposes a timeline, researches vendors, writes the weighted rubric, and generates the questionnaire — all editable before anything is created.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Silent RFP" icon="user-secret">
    Compare the field before contacting anyone. Coverbase researches how each shortlisted vendor would most plausibly answer, from public information, so ten candidates become three without spending anyone's goodwill.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Evidence-backed scoring" icon="shield-check">
    Every AI score cites the verbatim span of the response it rests on, sits beside the reviewer's own score rather than replacing it, and fails a must-have on ambiguity rather than passing it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Comparison and total cost" icon="table-columns">
    Compare bidders across weighted criteria, with year-one and three-year cost side by side and a badge for how firm each number is — a published price, a quote, an indicative range, or withheld.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Decision memo" icon="file-signature">
    After the award, a record of why: the reasoning, each runner-up and the specific reason it lost, the risks knowingly accepted, and the conditions the winner must meet before signature.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Selection-to-onboarding handoff" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Carry the winning bidder's responses and evidence straight into intake and assessment, with no re-keying of what was already collected.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Common workflows

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Risk-weighted vendor selection" defaultOpen icon="scale-balanced">
    Evaluate bidders on capability and cost alongside security, privacy, legal, and compliance posture in one scorecard, so a cheaper bidder with unacceptable risk doesn't win by default.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Selection-to-assessment continuity" icon="diagram-project">
    When a winner is chosen, push their RFP responses and evidence into [Autonomous Intake](/products/autonomous-intake) and [Assessment Copilot](/products/assessment-copilot) so onboarding starts from what's already known.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Regulated-industry sourcing" icon="building-columns">
    Run sourcing events that satisfy regulatory expectations for documented, criteria-based vendor selection, with a defensible audit trail of how each decision was made.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
